### Chess

- **[study-templates](https://github.com/fcardinaux/study-templates)** — blank chess
  boards with room to write, as vector PDFs for e-ink note apps and print.
  [Pick and download them here.](https://fcardinaux.github.io/study-templates/)
- **[chessfold](https://github.com/fcardinaux/chessfold)** — a chess move generator
  in Erlang.
- **[chess-move-generator.js](https://github.com/fcardinaux/chess-move-generator.js)**
  — a chess move generator in JavaScript, for Node.js and the browser.
